# Where It Breaks

1. "Exactly what date will I receive pending settlements if I close on 28 August 2026?"
   - The assistant can say "within 10 working days," but it should not be trusted to compute a date because the documents mention bank holidays and do not provide a holiday calendar.

2. "I am a grocery merchant with Rs.9.5 lakh volume this month and Rs.11 lakh last month. Which MDR applies?"
   - The documents do not define how monthly volume is measured, which month is used, or whether volume is projected or historical.

3. "Can a customer split a Rs.2,00,000 insurance payment into two UPI payments from two different bank accounts?"
   - The policy speaks about per-transaction limits and daily limits per customer account, but not customer identity across multiple accounts or split-payment anti-circumvention rules.
